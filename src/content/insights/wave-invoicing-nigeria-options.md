Wave has been a favourite of Nigerian freelancers and agencies for years: free, simple and good-looking. In 2026 many Nigerian users logged in to find this notice: *users outside the US and Canada are no longer able to automatically send invoices or reminders via Wave.* Here's what it means and how to decide what to do.

## What exactly changed?

According to Wave's in-app notice to users outside the US and Canada:

- **Sending invoices from Wave** (email delivery from the platform) is no longer available.
- **Automatic payment reminders** are no longer available.
- Wave points affected users to a **partner offer from Zoho Books**.

Other Wave features were already limited to the US and Canada before this: **Wave Payments** (card and bank payments on invoices) and **Wave Payroll** don't support Nigerian businesses.

## What still works for Nigerian users?

You can still log in to your books, record transactions and view reports, and you can still **create invoices and share them yourself** (for example by downloading the PDF and emailing it). Check your own account, since features can change.

## What are my options?

| Option | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Stay on Wave and send invoices manually** | Low volume, bookkeeping mostly | No reminders, no online payments, extra admin for every invoice |
| **Move to a global accounting platform** (such as Wave's partner offer) | Companies with accountants used to that tool | Check support for Nigerian VAT and WHT on invoices, Nigerian payment gateways and PAYE before committing |
| **Spreadsheets** | Very early-stage businesses | No reminders, error-prone, painful at tax time |
| **A Nigeria-first platform** | Companies that want invoicing, local payments, payroll and tax in one place | Check it can export your data if you ever leave |

## What should I look for in a replacement?

Make a shortlist against the things that matter in Nigeria:

1. **Invoices that pass corporate AP**: RC number, TIN, client PO number, VAT at 7.5% on its own line, WHT deduction, amount in words.
2. **Local payments**: Paystack or Flutterwave, bank transfer details, USSD, paid into **your** account.
3. **Automatic reminders**, since chasing is where cash flow is won or lost.
4. **Payroll with 2026 PAYE**, pension and payslips, if you have staff.
5. **Tax tracking** for VAT, PAYE, WHT and pension deadlines.
6. **Reports your accountant needs**: P&L, cash flow, aged receivables and payables, and a transactions export.
7. **Data export**, so you're never locked in.

## How do I move my data?

1. Export **customers**, **invoices** and **transactions** from Wave as CSV (Wave's reports and data export tools).
2. Note which **invoices are still unpaid**. Recreate those first so you can keep collecting.
3. Start the new tool from a clean date, such as the first day of the next month, and keep the old data for reference.
4. Tell clients if your **payment details or invoice format** change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I still use Wave in Nigeria?

Yes, for bookkeeping and creating invoices, but Wave no longer sends invoices or reminders automatically for users outside the US and Canada.

### Does Wave support Paystack or Flutterwave?

No. Wave Payments is available only in the US and Canada and doesn't integrate Nigerian gateways.

### Does Wave Payroll work in Nigeria?

No. Wave Payroll is for US and Canadian businesses.

### How do I export my data from Wave?

Use Wave's export tools to download customers, invoices and transactions as CSV files, then import or recreate them in your new tool, starting with unpaid invoices.
