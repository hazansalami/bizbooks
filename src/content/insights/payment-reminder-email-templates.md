Most unpaid invoices aren't disputes. They're forgotten. These templates are ready to copy. Replace the bracketed text, keep them short, and always make it easy to pay.

## Rules for every reminder

- **One invoice per email** (or a clear list if there are several)
- **Invoice number, amount, due date** in the first two lines
- **A way to pay** right there: payment link and bank details
- **A question that needs an answer**: "Can you confirm the payment date?"
- **Send to accounts payable**, copying your project contact

## 1. Three days before the due date

**Subject:** Invoice [INV-0042] due [Friday 2 Nov]

> Hi [Name],
>
> A quick heads-up that invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] is due on [Friday, 2 November].
>
> You can pay online here: [link], or by transfer to [Bank, account number, account name], using [INV-0042] as the reference.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]

## 2. On the due date

**Subject:** Invoice [INV-0042] is due today

> Hi [Name],
>
> Invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] is due today. I've attached it again for convenience.
>
> Pay online: [link] · Bank: [details]
>
> If it's already in your payment run, please ignore this. Thank you!

## 3. Seven days late

**Subject:** Invoice [INV-0042]: 7 days overdue

> Hi [Name],
>
> Invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] was due on [2 November] and is now 7 days overdue.
>
> Could you confirm when it's scheduled for payment? If anything is missing for your process (a PO number, a document, a vendor form), tell me and I'll send it today.

## 4. Fourteen days late (to your senior contact)

**Subject:** Overdue invoice [INV-0042] for [Client company]

> Hi [Senior contact],
>
> I wanted to flag that invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250], for [project/retainer], is now 14 days overdue. Our accounts contact hasn't been able to confirm a payment date.
>
> Could you help us get it into this week's payment run? Happy to jump on a quick call if that's easier.

## 5. Thirty days late (formal)

**Subject:** Payment required: invoice [INV-0042], 30 days overdue

> Dear [Senior contact],
>
> Invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] is now 30 days overdue. Under clause [X] of our agreement dated [date], invoices are payable within [30] days.
>
> Please arrange payment by [date, about 7 days from now]. If payment isn't received by then, we'll need to pause work on [project] until the account is up to date.
>
> We value working with [Client] and would like to resolve this quickly.

## 6. Final notice

**Subject:** Final notice: invoice [INV-0042]

> Dear [Name],
>
> Despite our previous reminders, invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] remains unpaid, [45] days after its due date. Work on [project] has been paused as notified on [date].
>
> Unless payment is received by [date], we will take further steps to recover the amount, which may include formal legal action, without further notice.

Only send this if you're prepared to follow through. A lawyer's letter at this stage often works.

## WhatsApp version (short and polite)

> Good morning [Name], a quick reminder that invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] was due on [2 Nov]. You can pay here: [link]. Please let me know when it's scheduled. Thank you!

## Subject lines that get opened

- "Invoice [number] due [date]" (clear beats clever)
- "Quick question about invoice [number]"
- "[Client]: overdue invoice [number]"

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I politely ask for payment?

Be brief and specific: invoice number, amount, due date, a way to pay, and a request to confirm the payment date. Offer to send anything missing.

### When should I send a payment reminder?

A few days before the due date, on the due date, then at 7, 14 and 30 days late, escalating in tone and seniority.

### Can I send payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes, many Nigerian businesses do. Keep it short and polite, and include the invoice number, amount and a payment link.

### What should a final notice include?

The invoice details, the history of reminders, the contract clause, a clear final payment date, and the specific next step you'll take.
