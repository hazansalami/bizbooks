"Please send your TIN" is one of the first things a corporate client's finance team asks for. The rules changed in 2026, and for most companies getting one is now simpler.

## What is a Tax ID?

A **Tax ID** (formerly the Taxpayer Identification Number, or TIN) is the number that identifies a taxpayer to the Nigeria Revenue Service and State Internal Revenue Services. Under the **Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025**, Nigeria moved to a **single, unified Tax ID** that replaces separate federal and state TINs.

## What is my company's Tax ID?

| Taxpayer | Tax ID from 2026 |
|---|---|
| Registered company | The **CAC RC number** |
| Business name | The **CAC BN number** |
| Individual | The **National Identification Number (NIN)** |

If your company already had a TIN, it's linked to the unified system. Check that your details on the tax portal match your CAC records exactly: name, RC number and address.

## Where should I use it?

- **On every invoice**, next to your company name and RC number
- In **vendor registration** forms for clients
- On **VAT returns**, WHT remittances and other filings
- When opening **bank accounts** and applying for loans
- On **payslips and PAYE schedules** as the employer identifier

## Why does it matter so much?

1. **VAT invoices.** Clients need your Tax ID on the invoice to claim input VAT. Without it, their finance team may send the invoice back.
2. **Withholding tax.** Small companies don't have to deduct WHT from a supplier with a **valid Tax ID** when the transaction is ₦2m or less in the month, so your Tax ID can mean you're paid in full. See [withholding tax in Nigeria](/insights/withholding-tax-in-nigeria).
3. **E-invoicing.** The NRS e-invoicing system identifies buyers and sellers by Tax ID. See [e-invoicing in Nigeria](/insights/e-invoicing-nigeria-mbs).
4. **Tax clearance.** Your filing history is tracked against it. See [tax clearance certificates](/insights/tax-clearance-certificate-nigeria).

## Should I also collect clients' Tax IDs?

Yes. For B2B VAT invoices, record each client's Tax ID (their RC number for companies). It makes your VAT records complete and prepares you for e-invoicing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my RC number my TIN?

From 2026, a registered company's CAC RC number serves as its Tax ID under the unified system. Business names use their BN number.

### Do I still need a separate TIN?

Generally no for newly registered entities, but check the tax portal to confirm your details are linked and correct.

### Why do clients ask for my TIN?

They need it to process VAT invoices, apply the right withholding tax treatment, and complete vendor registration.

### What is the Tax ID for individuals?

An individual's National Identification Number (NIN) serves as their Tax ID.
