PAYE (Pay As You Earn) is the income tax you deduct from employees' salaries and pay to the state tax authority. The 2026 reforms changed the calculation completely: the Consolidated Relief Allowance is gone, there's a tax-free band, and rent relief is new. Here's how to do it by hand, with four worked examples. Or use our free [PAYE calculator](/tools/paye-calculator).

## What are the PAYE tax bands in 2026?

Under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, from 1 January 2026:

| Annual taxable income | Rate | Maximum tax in band |
|---|---|---|
| ₦0 – ₦800,000 | **0%** | ₦0 |
| ₦800,001 – ₦3,000,000 | **15%** | ₦330,000 |
| ₦3,000,001 – ₦12,000,000 | **18%** | ₦1,620,000 |
| ₦12,000,001 – ₦25,000,000 | **21%** | ₦2,730,000 |
| ₦25,000,001 – ₦50,000,000 | **23%** | ₦5,750,000 |
| Above ₦50,000,000 | **25%** | – |

Tax is **progressive**: each rate applies only to the part of income inside that band.

## How do I calculate PAYE step by step?

1. **Annual gross pay**: monthly gross (basic + housing + transport + other taxable allowances) × 12.
2. **Subtract pension**: the employee's 8% contribution. Strictly this is 8% of basic + housing + transport.
3. **Subtract NHF** if the employee is registered: 2.5% of basic salary.
4. **Subtract rent relief**: 20% of the rent the employee actually pays each year, capped at **₦500,000**. The employee should give you evidence.
5. The result is **taxable income**. Apply the bands above.
6. **Divide by 12** for monthly PAYE.

## Worked example 1: ₦500,000 a month, pays ₦1.2m rent

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual gross (₦500,000 × 12) | ₦6,000,000 |
| Less pension (8%) | −₦480,000 |
| Less rent relief (20% × ₦1,200,000) | −₦240,000 |
| **Taxable income** | **₦5,280,000** |
| Tax: 0% on first ₦800,000 | ₦0 |
| 15% on next ₦2,200,000 | ₦330,000 |
| 18% on remaining ₦2,280,000 | ₦410,400 |
| **Annual PAYE** | **₦740,400** |
| **Monthly PAYE** | **₦61,700** |
| Monthly take-home (₦500,000 − ₦40,000 pension − ₦61,700) | **₦398,300** |

## Worked example 2: ₦250,000 a month, no rent relief claimed

Annual gross ₦3,000,000 − pension ₦240,000 = taxable **₦2,760,000**.
Tax = 15% × (₦2,760,000 − ₦800,000) = **₦294,000 a year = ₦24,500 a month**.
Take-home = ₦250,000 − ₦20,000 − ₦24,500 = **₦205,500**.

## Worked example 3: minimum wage (₦70,000 a month)

Annual gross ₦840,000 − pension ₦67,200 = taxable **₦772,800**, which is below ₦800,000.
**PAYE is ₦0.** Take-home = ₦70,000 − ₦5,600 pension = **₦64,400**.

## Worked example 4: ₦950,000 a month, pays ₦2.4m rent

Annual gross ₦11,400,000 − pension ₦912,000 − rent relief ₦480,000 = taxable **₦10,008,000**.
Tax = ₦330,000 + 18% × (₦10,008,000 − ₦3,000,000) = ₦330,000 + ₦1,261,440 = **₦1,591,440 a year = ₦132,620 a month**.

## Who gets the PAYE?

PAYE goes to the **State Internal Revenue Service of the state where the employee lives**, not necessarily where your office is. If your Lagos company employs someone who lives in Ogun State, their PAYE goes to Ogun. Remit by the **10th of the following month**, and file the annual employer return (**Form H1**) by 31 January.

## Common PAYE mistakes

- **Still applying the old Consolidated Relief Allowance.** It was abolished from 2026.
- **Giving rent relief without evidence.** Keep a tenancy agreement or rent receipt on file for each claim.
- **Taxing contractors through PAYE.** Independent contractors are usually subject to [withholding tax](/insights/withholding-tax-in-nigeria), not PAYE. See [employee or contractor?](/insights/employee-or-contractor-nigeria)
- **Remitting to the wrong state.** PAYE follows the employee's residence.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the tax-free threshold in Nigeria in 2026?

The first ₦800,000 of annual taxable income is taxed at 0% under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025.

### Is the Consolidated Relief Allowance still used?

No. The CRA was removed from 1 January 2026. Rent relief (20% of annual rent, up to ₦500,000) and other specific reliefs such as pension and NHF apply instead.

### Is pension deducted before PAYE is calculated?

Yes. The employee's pension contribution is deducted from gross income before applying the tax bands, which reduces PAYE.

### Do minimum wage earners pay PAYE in 2026?

Generally no. At ₦70,000 a month, taxable income after pension is below the ₦800,000 tax-free threshold.

### What is the highest PAYE rate in Nigeria?

25%, on annual taxable income above ₦50 million.
