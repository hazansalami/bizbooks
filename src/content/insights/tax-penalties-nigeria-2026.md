Penalties are the most avoidable cost a company pays. Here's what late filing and late payment actually cost under the 2026 rules, and a simple routine to never pay them.

## What is the penalty for filing late?

Under the **Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 (NTAA)**, failing to file a return on time attracts an administrative penalty of:

- **₦100,000 for the first month** of default, and
- **₦50,000 for each subsequent month** (or part of a month) the return is outstanding.

**Example:** A company files its annual return 5 months late: ₦100,000 + (4 × ₦50,000) = **₦300,000**, even if the tax due is small.

## What is the penalty for paying late?

Late payment of tax due attracts:

- a **penalty of 10%** of the unpaid amount, **plus**
- **interest** at the Central Bank of Nigeria's **monetary policy rate plus a spread**, running until the tax is paid.

**Example:** ₦2,000,000 VAT paid two months late costs a ₦200,000 penalty plus two months of interest.

## What about pension, PAYE and CAC?

| Obligation | Consequence of lateness |
|---|---|
| **Pension** remittance (due within 7 working days of pay day) | Penalty of **at least 2% of the outstanding contribution per month** under the Pension Reform Act 2014, plus PenCom recovery action |
| **PAYE** (due by the 10th to the State IRS) | State penalties and interest, and the amounts are employees' money held in trust |
| **CAC annual returns** | Late filing penalties, and under CAMA 2020 directors can be personally liable |

## What else does non-compliance cost?

- **No tax clearance certificate**, which can cost you a government or corporate contract. See [how to get a TCC](/insights/tax-clearance-certificate-nigeria).
- **Audits and best-of-judgement assessments**, where the tax authority estimates your liability if you don't file.
- **Bank and vendor-registration friction**, since more clients now check tax status.

## How do I avoid penalties?

1. **Put every deadline in one calendar**: PAYE on the 10th, VAT and WHT on the 21st, pension within 7 working days, annual returns within 6 months of year end. See the [2026 tax calendar](/insights/nigeria-tax-calendar-2026).
2. **File even when you owe nothing.** Nil VAT returns and 0% company returns still have to be filed.
3. **Set tax money aside when you get paid**, not when it's due.
4. **Keep proof of every payment and remittance.**
5. **If you're already late, file and pay now.** Penalties and interest keep growing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the penalty for late filing of tax returns in Nigeria?

₦100,000 for the first month and ₦50,000 for each subsequent month under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025.

### What is the penalty for late payment of tax?

A 10% penalty on the unpaid amount plus interest based on the CBN monetary policy rate, until the tax is paid.

### What happens if I remit pension late?

The Pension Reform Act 2014 imposes a penalty of at least 2% of the outstanding contribution for each month of default, and PenCom can take recovery action.

### Do I get penalised for a late nil return?

Yes. The filing penalty applies to late returns even when no tax is due, so file nil returns on time.
