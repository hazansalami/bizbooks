For most Nigerian agencies the problem isn't winning clients, it's **getting paid by them**. A single large client paying 90 days late can mean salaries paid from an overdraft, VAT paid on money you haven't received, and a founder spending Fridays on the phone. Collections isn't about being aggressive. It's about **a system that runs whether or not you feel like chasing**.

## Why do clients pay late?

In most Nigerian corporate payables processes, late payment has boring causes:

- **The invoice is stuck.** No PO number, missing TIN, wrong entity name, sent to the wrong person.
- **Approval chains.** The invoice needs sign-off from someone who's travelling.
- **Payment runs.** Many companies pay suppliers only once or twice a month.
- **Nobody asked.** Invoices without follow-up sink to the bottom of the pile.
- **Cash-flow games.** Some clients deliberately stretch suppliers. A system protects you here too.

Fix the first four and the fifth becomes visible.

## Step 1: Prevent late payment in the contract

- **Payment terms in writing**: 14 or 30 days from invoice date, not "on completion".
- **A deposit or mobilisation fee** on project work. See [mobilisation fees and deposits](/insights/mobilisation-fees-and-deposits).
- **Milestone billing** for projects longer than a month.
- **A late-payment clause.** Even if you rarely enforce it, it sets expectations.
- **The right to pause work** when invoices are more than a set number of days overdue.
- **Get the client's vendor onboarding done before you start**: vendor registration, PO process and accounts contact.

## Step 2: Send invoices they can actually process

Every invoice should carry the client's **PO number**, both companies' **TINs**, VAT shown separately and the correct legal entity name. Send it to their **accounts payable email** and copy your project contact. Use our [invoice checklist](/insights/how-to-write-a-professional-invoice-in-nigeria).

## Step 3: Follow a reminder schedule

| When | Channel | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days before due | Email | Friendly heads-up with the payment link |
| Due date | Email | "Due today" with invoice attached |
| 3 days late | Email + call to accounts | Check it's in the payment run |
| 7 days late | Email to accounts, copy your contact | Firm, ask for a payment date |
| 14 days late | Call your senior contact | Escalate the relationship, not the tone |
| 30 days late | Formal letter | Reference contract terms, late fees, work pause |
| 60+ days late | Pause work | Stop new work until the account is current |

## Reminder templates you can use

**Before due date**
> Hi [Name], a quick note that invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] is due on [2 November]. You can pay by transfer to [bank details] or online here: [link]. Thank you.

**7 days late**
> Hi [Name], invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] was due on [2 November] and is now 7 days overdue. Could you confirm the date it's scheduled for payment? If anything is missing for your process (PO, documents), tell me and I'll send it today.

**30 days late**
> Dear [Senior contact], invoice [INV-0042] for [₦1,281,250] is now 30 days overdue. Under clause [X] of our agreement, invoices are payable within [30] days. Please arrange payment by [date]. Otherwise we'll need to pause work on [project] until the account is up to date. We value this relationship and would rather resolve this quickly.

## Step 4: Make paying easy

- A **"Pay now" link** on the invoice for card, transfer or USSD
- Your **account number with a copy button**
- A clear **reference** (the invoice number) so you can match the payment
- A way for the client to say **"we've paid"**, so you're not chasing money that has already left their account

## Step 5: Watch your aged receivables every week

Look at **who owes you, and for how long** every Monday: coming due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days. The 90+ bucket is the one that turns into bad debt. The earlier you act, the more you collect.

## What if a client still won't pay?

After 60–90 days, and with your contract in hand: stop further work, send a formal demand letter (a lawyer's letter often works), and consider negotiated instalments rather than losing everything. Keep emails and delivery evidence organised from the start.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I politely remind a client to pay?

Send a short, specific message with the invoice number, amount, due date and a way to pay, and ask them to confirm the payment date. Offer to send anything missing for their process.

### When should I stop work for non-payment?

Set the point in your contract. Many agencies pause new work once an invoice is 60 days overdue, after written warnings at 14 and 30 days.

### Should I charge late payment interest?

Include a late-payment clause in contracts. Whether you enforce it depends on the relationship, but having it gives you leverage.

### What payment terms are normal in Nigeria?

For corporate clients, 14–30 days is common, with deposits or milestone payments on projects.
