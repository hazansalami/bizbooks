If your profit looks good but cash is tight, the aged receivables report usually explains why. It's the most actionable report a service business has.

## What is an aged receivables report?

An **aged receivables** (or accounts receivable aging) report lists every **unpaid client invoice**, grouped by **how long it's been overdue**:

| Bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Coming due** | Not yet due |
| **1–30 days** | Recently overdue, usually an admin delay |
| **31–60 days** | A pattern is forming |
| **61–90 days** | Serious. The client may have cash problems or a dispute |
| **90+ days** | At risk of never being paid |

Most reports also show a **total per client**, so you can see who owes the most.

## How do I read it?

Ask three questions:

1. **How much is overdue in total?** Compare it with a month of your costs.
2. **Where is it concentrated?** One client with ₦20m in 90+ days is a bigger risk than ten small late payers.
3. **Is it getting better or worse?** Compare with last month.

## What is DSO?

**Days sales outstanding (DSO)** estimates how long, on average, clients take to pay:

**DSO = (receivables ÷ credit sales for the period) × days in the period**

**Example:** ₦24m receivables, ₦36m invoiced in the last 90 days → (24 ÷ 36) × 90 = **60 days**. If your terms are 30 days, clients are taking twice as long.

## What should I do about each bucket?

| Bucket | Action |
|---|---|
| Coming due | Make sure invoices are complete (PO, Tax ID) and sent to accounts payable |
| 1–30 days | Reminders at 3 and 7 days, ask for a payment date |
| 31–60 days | Call your senior contact, confirm there's no dispute, agree a date |
| 61–90 days | Formal letter citing the contract, pause new work |
| 90+ days | Final notice, lawyer's letter, negotiate instalments, consider write-off |

## When should I write off a bad debt?

When the debt is **genuinely irrecoverable**: the client has closed, disputes are lost, or recovery would cost more than the debt. Keep **evidence of your recovery efforts**. For tax, bad debts are generally only deductible when they're proven to be bad and written off, so talk to your accountant before year end.

## How do I stop invoices ageing in the first place?

- **Deposits** on projects and **retainers billed in advance**. See [mobilisation fees](/insights/mobilisation-fees-and-deposits).
- **Complete invoices** that pass corporate accounts payable. See the [invoice checklist](/insights/how-to-write-a-professional-invoice-in-nigeria).
- **Automatic reminders** so nothing sits unchased.
- **Weekly review** of this report.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an aged receivables report?

A report of unpaid client invoices grouped by how long they're overdue, typically coming due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days.

### What does 90+ days mean in aged receivables?

The invoices are more than 90 days past their due date. They're the least likely to be collected and need urgent action.

### How often should I review aged receivables?

Weekly. Acting on 30-day items is far more effective than chasing 90-day ones.

### How do I calculate DSO?

Divide receivables by credit sales for a period and multiply by the number of days in that period.
