Hiring your first employees is a milestone, and the start of monthly obligations to four or five different bodies. This guide lists what a Nigerian company must set up, what to deduct, what the company pays on top, and a routine that keeps you compliant.

## What must I register for before running payroll?

1. **State Internal Revenue Service (SIRS)**: register as an employer for PAYE in the state where your employees live (Lagos IRS, FCT IRS and so on). You'll need your company TIN.
2. **Pension**: under the Pension Reform Act 2014, employers with **3 or more employees** must operate the contributory pension scheme. Each employee chooses a Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) and has a **Retirement Savings Account (RSA) PIN**.
3. **NSITF (Employee Compensation Scheme)**: register and contribute **1% of monthly payroll**.
4. **ITF (Industrial Training Fund)**: employers with **5 or more employees** (or ₦50m+ turnover) contribute **1% of annual payroll**.
5. **NHF (National Housing Fund)**: employees who register contribute **2.5% of basic salary**, deducted by the employer.

## What do I deduct from each employee's pay?

| Deduction | Rate | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **PAYE** | Per the 2026 bands (first ₦800,000 a year tax-free) | State IRS where the employee lives |
| **Employee pension** | 8% of basic + housing + transport | Employee's PFA |
| **NHF** (if registered) | 2.5% of basic | Federal Mortgage Bank of Nigeria |

See [how to calculate PAYE](/insights/how-to-calculate-paye-in-nigeria) for worked examples.

## What does the company pay on top of salaries?

| Employer cost | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Employer pension** | 10% of basic + housing + transport |
| **NSITF** | 1% of monthly payroll |
| **ITF** | 1% of annual payroll (5+ staff or ₦50m+ turnover) |

**Example:** An employee on ₦400,000 gross costs the company about **₦444,000 a month**: ₦400,000 pay + ₦40,000 employer pension + ₦4,000 NSITF, plus ITF over the year.

## What's the monthly payroll routine?

1. **Before pay day:** collect changes (new hires, exits, raises, unpaid leave).
2. **Calculate** gross pay, PAYE, pension and NHF for each person.
3. **Pay net salaries** from the company account, usually by bulk transfer.
4. **Issue payslips.** Each one shows gross pay, every deduction and net pay.
5. **Remit pension** to each PFA **within 7 working days** of paying salaries.
6. **Remit PAYE** to the State IRS **by the 10th** of the next month.
7. **Record the cost** in your books: salaries, employer pension and statutory contributions.
8. **Keep records.** At year end you'll file the **employer's annual return (Form H1) by 31 January**.

## What should be on a payslip?

At minimum: company name, employee name and ID, pay period, gross pay broken down by component, each deduction (PAYE, pension, NHF, others), net pay and the pay date. Showing the **employer's pension contribution** too helps staff see their full package.

## Employees or contractors?

Freelancers and contractors are generally **not** on PAYE. You deduct [withholding tax](/insights/withholding-tax-in-nigeria) (usually 5% for professional services) instead. Treating someone who works like an employee as a contractor can create back-tax and pension liabilities. See [employee or contractor?](/insights/employee-or-contractor-nigeria)

## Frequently asked questions

### Is pension compulsory for small companies in Nigeria?

It's compulsory for employers with 3 or more employees under the Pension Reform Act 2014: 8% from the employee and 10% from the employer.

### When is PAYE remitted?

By the 10th of the month following the salary payment, to the State Internal Revenue Service where each employee lives.

### What is NSITF and how much is it?

The Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund runs the Employee Compensation Scheme. Employers contribute 1% of monthly payroll.

### Who pays ITF?

Employers with 5 or more employees, or turnover of ₦50 million or more, contribute 1% of annual payroll to the Industrial Training Fund.

### Is NHF compulsory?

NHF is 2.5% of basic salary for employees registered with the scheme. Employers deduct and remit it for registered staff.
