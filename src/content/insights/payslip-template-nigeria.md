A payslip is how employees check they've been paid correctly. It's also what banks, landlords and embassies ask for, and what auditors look at. A clear payslip saves HR questions every month.

## What should a Nigerian payslip include?

**Employer details**
- Company name, RC number / Tax ID, address

**Employee details**
- Full name, employee ID, job title or department
- RSA PIN and Pension Fund Administrator (if on pension)

**Pay period**
- Month covered and **payment date**

**Earnings**
- Basic salary
- Housing allowance
- Transport allowance
- Other allowances (utility, meal, leave, overtime, bonus), each on its own line
- **Gross pay**

**Deductions**
- **PAYE tax**
- **Employee pension (8%)**
- **NHF (2.5% of basic)**, if registered
- Other deductions (loan repayments, cooperative, advances), each labelled
- **Total deductions**

**Net pay**
- The amount paid, and the bank account it went to

**Good practice extras**
- **Employer pension (10%)**, so staff see their full package
- **Year-to-date** gross, PAYE and pension totals
- Rent relief applied, if any

## Common payslip mistakes

- **One "allowances" line.** Break them out. Pension depends on basic, housing and transport specifically.
- **Deductions without labels.** Staff will ask what each one is.
- **No pay date or period.** Banks and embassies reject these.
- **Numbers that don't match the bank transfer.** Net pay must equal what was actually paid.
- **Sending payslips as editable Word files.** Use PDF or a secure link.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the format of a payslip in Nigeria?

A payslip should show employer and employee details, the pay period and date, each earning, each deduction (PAYE, pension, NHF and others), gross pay and net pay.

### Must employers give payslips in Nigeria?

Giving each employee a clear statement of pay and deductions is standard practice, and banks, landlords and embassies routinely ask employees for recent payslips.

### Should the payslip show employer pension?

It isn't a deduction, but showing the employer's 10% contribution helps staff understand their full package.

### Can a payslip be sent by email?

Yes. A PDF or secure link is fine and is easier for staff to keep.
